      FAQ

      Something else? Email us at hola@facha.app.

      Do my clients need to download an app?

      No. They book from a link that opens in their phone's browser. Put it in your Instagram bio or send it on WhatsApp.

      When do I get the deposit money?

      It goes straight to your Mercado Pago account, on whatever payout schedule you have set there. We never touch that money.

      What happens if a client cancels?

      You set the rule. By default, cancelling more than 12 hours ahead gets the deposit back; if they don't show, you keep the deposit.

      How does Facha know who to message?

      It tracks each client's visits. If someone gets a cut every 4 weeks and it's been 5, it sends them a message with your link. You can pause it per client or turn it off entirely.

      Does it work with several barbers?

      Yes. Each barber has their own calendar and clients choose who they book with.

      How does the guarantee work?

      At the end of your first month on Pro, we add up deposits kept from no-shows and bookings that came from reactivation. If that doesn't exceed $14.900, we don't charge you for the month.

      How do you count a recovered client on Pay per result?

      Someone who hadn't been in for 4 weeks or more, got our reactivation message and booked within the next 14 days. Only those are charged, at $3.000 each, and you can see every one in the dashboard.

      Is there a minimum contract?

      No. Cancel from the dashboard whenever you like and you go back to the Free plan.
